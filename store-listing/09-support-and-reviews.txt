Support request template:

Subject: Session Switcher – multi-session issue

Please send:
• Extension version
• Chrome/Chromium browser and version
• Operating system
• Site category (for example, internal dashboard or webmail; do not send credentials)
• Number of sessions and tabs
• Exact steps and approximate time until the issue appeared
• Whether the computer slept or the browser was restarted
• Whether the issue affects cookies, localStorage/IndexedDB, or both

Never attach cookies, tokens, credentials, or exported session backups.
