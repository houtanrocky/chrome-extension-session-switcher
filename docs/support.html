Before submitting

  • Include the extension version, browser version, operating system, site category, and number of tabs.
  • Describe the exact steps and approximate time before the problem appeared.
  • Say whether the computer slept or the browser restarted.
  • Never include passwords, cookies, tokens, screenshots with personal data, or exported session backups.

Support tickets are handled through GitHub. You may need to sign in to submit one. For private account questions, email houtan.cs.0@gmail.com and do not attach credentials or session files.
